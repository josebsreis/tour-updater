Tour Updater

Replace, rename, reorder or add the rooms of a tour, then download it ready to upload. Everything happens on this computer — nothing is sent anywhere.

1 Open the tour

Drop the tour folder here
(the folder you upload to the server, with index.html inside)

2 Change the rooms

Drag a new panorama onto a room to replace its picture, or use Replace. Type to rename. Drag ⠿ or use the arrows to reorder — the first room is where the tour starts. A square top-bottom panorama (left eye on top) gives VR depth; a 2:1 panorama is flat.

    Add a room — drop a new panorama here, or

    3 Save the updated tour

    1. Drag the downloaded zip into the tour's folder on the server.
    2. Unzip it there, replacing the old files. The zip can be deleted afterwards.
    3. Open the tour link and step through every room. On a headset that saved the tour offline, press Update offline copy.

    Works with tours exported by Tour Studio. Tiles are made at the tour's own JPEG quality, at full resolution.
